# Squeekscope design

## Overview

Squeekscope is a small daily reading room for Ethereum addresses. Its visual idea is an ivory almanac with engraved hamster tarot illustrations. Black ink carries the reading, oxblood marks the draw action, and one flat gold appears in the artwork. The main reading is complete before the week, pairing and technical proof. On wide screens the draw form sits beside the reading; on a phone it follows the compact reading immediately.

The final implementation is defined by `src/style.css`, `src/main.tsx`, `src/deck.ts` and the SVGs generated by `scripts/generate-art.mjs`. Evidence and coverage limits are in `docs/validation.md`.

Measured WCAG contrast on the rendered CSS tokens: ink/page 13.54:1; muted/page 5.25:1; accent/page and page/primary-fill 8.31:1; muted/subtle 4.72:1; ink/field 14.54:1. The twelve lucky-colour swatches come from `COLOURS` in `src/deck.ts`; each has a visible name, so the reading never depends on identifying a coloured dot. Those swatches are data, rather than additional interface accent roles.

There is one light appearance. Forced-colors mode preserves system outlines and control boundaries. Unknown or stale data is stated in words; it is never conveyed by colour alone.

## Typography

- Display: locally bundled **Fraunces**, then Georgia/serif; normal weights 400–500, italic 400. Titles, card names and omens use this family.
- Body and controls: locally bundled **DM Sans**, then Arial/sans-serif; weights 400–600 are available; the implemented UI uses 400 and 500.
- Data: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace` for full addresses, hashes and formulas. Prose remains proportional.

The Latin WOFF2 files and their OFL licenses live in `public/fonts/`, copied to `dist/fonts/`. All required text and fonts are local; no font CDN is contacted at runtime.

The six-token scale is 14, 16, 20, 28, 40 and 64 px at the default root size, expressed in rem. Phone headings use the 40 px step, section/card names use 28 px, and inputs stay at 16 px. Display tracking is −0.045em; section tracking is −0.025em; body tracking is natural. There are no tracked capital kickers. Body line height is 1.5; heading line height is 1.12; audit rows use 1.6 and the formula block 1.8. Changing numeric values use tabular figures.

Headings balance their lines. Long prose is bounded around 65–75 characters on wide screens. Full hashes and addresses wrap with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` and remain selectable. Native address inputs scroll their editable value; the complete subject is also available in the proof. Small text stays at weight 400 or above.

## Layout

The centred `.site-shell` has a 1328 px maximum outer width and 56 px inner gutters on desktop. Most grouping uses space: 8–12 px inside controls, 16–24 px within reading groups, and 28–44 px between sections. Structural rules mark the almanac header, dateline, facts, week and proof.

The wide `.main-grid` uses a 1.85:1 reading/form ratio with a 52 px gap. The reading has an art/copy grid and three facts below it. The large illustration caps at 236 px wide. The initial form contains address, UTC date, one filled primary action and an optional wallet utility.

Content-driven breakpoints in `src/style.css`:

| Breakpoint | Implemented behavior |
| --- | --- |
| 76rem | Gutters reduce to 36 px; main gap to 30 px; reading text compacts |
| 58rem | Reading and form stack; gutters 28 px; deck becomes three columns; mood becomes a small inline row |
| 40rem | Gutters 20 px; header wraps if text grows; hero 40 px; illustration 104 px beside copy; deck two columns; pair results stack |
| 23rem | Gutters 16 px; illustration 94 px; date and draw button stack |

On phones, the reading uses flex wrapping with an `11em` copy basis. This keeps the ordinary view compact and lets enlarged text move under the art. Form headings and controls can wrap. The week has seven columns on desktop and automatically fits columns with a 4.5rem minimum on phones: four then three at 390 px, three columns at 320 px, and two columns under the checked 200% text enlargement. There is no hidden horizontal carousel.

Observed widths: 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px. Both open and closed proof states reflowed without horizontal scroll. The default 390 × 844 landing's primary button occupies y=769.39–816.39 px. At 200% root text size the 390 px page retained a 390 px scroll width. Browser-native zoom and physical devices were not tested.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. There are no drop shadows, gradients, glowing surfaces or entrance animations. Ivory tone changes group the form; one-pixel rules carry structure. The SVG tarot frames are part of the illustrations, rather than nested UI cards. The native wallet dialog alone uses a translucent dark backdrop and the browser's modal layer.

## Shapes

Controls use 3 px radii, the wallet dialog 4 px, and vector tarot stock 9 px in a 360 × 500 view box. Named colour dots are circular with a thin outline. Buttons have at least 46 px minimum height, while quiet utilities retain their text-link shape and dedicated spacing. The wallet utility is 32 px high on the phone layout, above the required target minimum; primary actions remain at least 46 px.

| `Deck` | `src/main.tsx:1146` | Static, fully readable gallery of all 22 artworks and both interpretations; a real return link replaces unnecessary selection controls |
| Buttons/fields | `src/style.css` | One filled oxblood primary action; neutral secondaries; hover, pressed, disabled and keyboard focus states |

Native links, buttons, forms, date input, `details` and `dialog` carry browser keyboard behavior. A skip link precedes the header. Focus uses a 2 px outline with 4 px offset, with system colours in forced-colors mode. There is no autoplay or continuous motion. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, buttons receive named 120 ms background/border/transform transitions and a 0.96 press scale; reduced motion removes them.

Loading preserves the last complete snapshot when possible. A failed public read offers retry and an explicit timestamp. A missing genesis anchor never produces a substitute reading. PNG layout uses the same stock, ink, oxblood, gold, fonts and art in `src/save-image.ts` at 1080 × 1350 px.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Start additional views inside `.site-shell` and reuse the existing typography, section-heading, field and button roles.
- Keep one filled primary action in each view. Use quiet utilities for sharing, wallet address fill and data refresh.
- Preserve the fixed deck/colour order and keep mood independent of the draw. Show actual provenance and provisional state wherever a reading appears.
- Let hashes and long addresses wrap; keep the full value selectable. Let enlarged text add height and reduce columns.
- Keep illustrations to ivory, black ink, oxblood and flat gold; lucky-colour dots remain named data.
- Add new UI states with native semantics and the existing focus treatment. Do not add motion, wallet signing, chain switching or a backend to this product.
